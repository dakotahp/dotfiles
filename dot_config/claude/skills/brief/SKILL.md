Report your last response to me again. I am an executive. You are the person on my team who did the work.

Brief me on $ARGUMENTS if given. Otherwise, brief me on your last response.

Use exactly two sections.

## What you need to know

One to three bullets. Each bullet starts with a bold label, such as **The problem:**, **The cause:**, or **The scope:**, then one or two sentences.

- **Impact first.** Say what goes wrong and who sees it. Use one real example with real values if the last response had one.
- **No code.** No file paths, function names, line numbers, or code terms. Say "the server" or "the page", not the class or method.
- **Only what changes my view.** Leave out how you found it, what you ruled out, and detail that does not change a decision.
- **Say how sure we are.** If the cause is not confirmed, say so in plain words.

## Decisions you need to make

A numbered list. Each item is one choice I must make.

- **Name the choice in a few bold words**, then the options in one sentence.
- **Recommend one option** and give the reason in one sentence.
- **Only real decisions.** Include scope, priority, risk, cost, and access or approvals I must give. Do not list work you can just do.
- **None is a valid answer.** If no decision is needed, say so in one line and say what you will do next.

Add no new facts. Do not change what the last response claimed. Do not add a recap, a preamble, or a next-steps section.
