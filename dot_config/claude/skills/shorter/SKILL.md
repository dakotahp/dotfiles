Cut your last response in half. Keep the point.

Shorten $ARGUMENTS if given. Otherwise, shorten your last response to me.

- **Half or less.** The new response has at most half the words of the last one.
- **Lead with the point.** The first sentence is the conclusion, the fix, or the direct answer.
- **Cut, do not compress.** Remove whole sentences and sections that do not carry the point. Do not pack the same content into denser wording.
- **Keep what I need to act.** Keep any real risk, failing test, wrong assumption, or exact command, path, or value. Cut background, reasoning, examples, and caveats first.
- **Same facts.** Add nothing new. Do not change what the last response claimed.
- **No meta.** Do not say that this is a shorter version, or list what you removed.

If cutting to half drops something that matters, end with one line naming it.
